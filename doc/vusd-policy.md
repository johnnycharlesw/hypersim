# VUSD policy

First of all, let's start with what VUSD is and isn't.
## What VUSD is
VUSD is:
* Hypersim's virtual currency
* Obtained via gifts from NPCs
* Purely in-game

## What VUSD is not
VUSD is not:
* A mask for real-world currency (looking at you, Fortnite!)
* Exchangable for real-world money
* Able to be spawned in 10000 times to gain obscene amounts of in-game wealth
