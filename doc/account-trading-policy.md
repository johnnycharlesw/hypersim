# Account trading policy
While gift cards and in-game items can be traded, you are not allowed to trade fully activated accounts.  

"Trading accounts" includes, but is not limited to:
* Buying an account that was created by someone else
* Selling an account to someone else
* Developing or using any "username sniper" in order to force a username onto any account
* Running giveaways for accounts
* Sharing the credentials for your account with anyone else intentionally, with verifiable proof.
